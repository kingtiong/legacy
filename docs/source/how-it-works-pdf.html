BNB Smart Chain

How the system works

A plain-language guide to what has been built: the four smart contracts, what each one does, how they are connected, and the website and helper around them.

Status as of 15 September 2026 · Deployed 14 September 2026 · Source: github.com/kingtiong/legacy (commit 6e8250a)

Deposits paused for a security upgrade. A pre-audit review (16 September 2026) found an issue that could freeze withdrawals in the contracts below. They hold no deposits. A fixed version will be deployed at new addresses before deposits reopen; until then, do not send BNB to these addresses.

The big picture

The four smart contracts

1Vault: the savings box

Holds everyone's BNB, locks it, stakes it, pays it back.

0x0C09EC94aDb65314448562B028FC5AfDBa421742

  • Deposit 0.01 to 10 BNB; it joins this month's "rung".
  • At least 70% goes to retirement (can never be sold or moved); up to 30% to emergency (sellable only through the market).
  • You receive shares: your receipt, growing in value as rewards come in.
  • BNB is staked with BNB Chain validators. You keep 70% of rewards; 30% go to the DAO treasury.
  • Each rung unlocks after 10 years. Then claim all or part: paid instantly, or after about 7 days of unstaking.
  • Your shares are also your votes in the DAO.
  • Safety caps: pool limited to 100 BNB, rising 50 BNB a month; stake moves between validators at most 10% a week.

2Market: the emergency exit

The only way to turn emergency shares into cash before they unlock.

0xCb4f25dD8E185e7B698a0DD48fE2b15861fAC8F1

  • A buyer locks USDT or USDC in an offer: "I pay X for Y emergency shares".
  • You accept; your shares wait inside the market.
  • You have 7 days to change your mind and cancel.
  • After 7 days, the buyer collects the shares and you collect the money, each separately, so neither side can block the other.
  • Never touches retirement shares. Trading closes 7 days before a rung unlocks.
  • No owner and no fees.

3Treasury: the DAO's wallet

Where the protocol's income goes, controlled only by depositor votes.

0x81D43e758CC917802DeFeA3A290D138302B8b8A2

  • Receives the protocol's 30% share of staking rewards.
  • Holds the power to add or remove validators (the "curator" role).
  • Does nothing unless a DAO vote passes, and then only after a 2-day waiting period, so everyone can see what is coming.
  • No admin key: not the team, not anyone.
  • No spending limit: a passed vote can spend all of it.

4Governor: the voting booth

Where depositors propose and vote.

0xE398C073F29CcdaF5c03a0C19E3147c13B25991A

  • Anyone holding shares can vote. 1 share = 1 vote.
  • Votes are counted as they stood when the proposal was created, so deposits or purchases made afterwards cannot swing it.
  • Creating a proposal needs a ladder worth about 1 BNB.
  • Voting lasts 7 days. It passes with more "for" than "against" and at least 10% of all votes taking part.
  • A passed proposal goes to the treasury, waits 2 days, then anyone can carry it out.

How the contracts are connected

LinkWhat it means
Vault → ValidatorsYour BNB is staked with BNB Chain validators to earn rewards, and comes back from them when you claim.
Vault ↔ MarketThe market is the only contract allowed to move emergency shares, and only before they unlock. The vault refuses to move retirement shares for anyone, ever.
Vault → Treasury30% of rewards are paid to the treasury as fee shares. The treasury is also the curator that chooses validators.
Governor → VaultThe governor reads everyone's shares to count votes. It cannot change them.
Governor → TreasuryThe governor is the only one allowed to give the treasury orders, and only for proposals that passed.
Treasury → VaultThe treasury carries out passed votes: spend fee income, add or remove validators, move stake within the 10% weekly limit.

These links were fixed at deployment. Each contract checked its partners before it would deploy (for example, the market refused to exist unless the vault expected exactly that market). None of them can be changed afterwards.

A deposit's life, step by step

StepWhat happens
1. DepositYou send BNB from the website. The vault gives you retirement and emergency shares for this month.
2. StakeOnce 1 BNB or more is waiting, the keeper (or anyone) presses "stake" and the BNB goes to a validator.
3. EarnRewards raise the value of every share. 30% of the gain goes to the DAO treasury.
4. VoteWhile you hold shares, you can vote on how fees are spent and which validators are used.
5. (Optional) SellIf life happens, sell emergency shares on the market, with 7 days to cancel.
6. Unlock and claimAfter 10 years, claim all or part. The BNB is paid instantly or after about 7 days of unstaking.

Around the contracts

Website

decadium.club/app. Four screens:

My ladder: your rungs, values, unlock dates and claims.
Deposit: amount, split, and a plain summary before you confirm.
Market: offers, selling with cooling-off, buying.
DAO: treasury, proposals, voting.

Keeper (auto-staking helper)

0xB888df3068230B1dEaCd56F5D259D6BD0661B7E5

Runs on the server. Every 10 minutes it checks the vault and presses "stake" when 1 BNB or more is waiting, paying about 0.00003 BNB in fees from its own wallet. It has no role in any contract and cannot move anyone's money. If it stops, anyone can press "Stake it now" on the website.

Open source and verification

All code: github.com/kingtiong/legacy. All four contracts are verified on Sourcify as an exact match, so anyone can confirm the code running on BNB Chain is the code on GitHub. Tested with 110 unit and fuzz tests and 16 tests against a copy of BNB Chain mainnet.

What nobody can ever do

Not you, not the team, not the builder, not a DAO vote:
  • Take or move anyone's deposit
  • Unlock a rung before its 10 years are up
  • Stop someone from claiming after unlock
  • Change or upgrade the contracts

Known risks

RiskWhat it means
Not auditedA bug could cause losses shared by all depositors. Deposit caps keep the early amount small.
BNB priceIf BNB falls, every ladder falls with it. Nothing guarantees a return.
DAO captureWith low turnout, a large holder could pass a vote to spend the treasury or pick poor validators. Deposits stay out of reach.
BNB Chain changesBNB Chain controls its staking system. A breaking change there could affect staking for everyone.
Lost wallet keysThere is no recovery. Whoever holds the wallet key owns the ladder.
